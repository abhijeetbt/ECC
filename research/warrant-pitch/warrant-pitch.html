The Solution

A background-check service for everything your AI agents install and run.

Warrant scans every plugin, tool connection, and configuration your company's AI agents use — across every AI tool your teams already have, not just one — and gives security leaders one dashboard with one clear answer: safe, risky, or blocked, and why.

Think of it as the inspection sticker in a restaurant window — the same idea, applied to the software your AI agents are about to run before it touches your codebase.
